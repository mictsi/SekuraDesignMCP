Questions people actually ask

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

The workspace becomes read-only and export stays enabled indefinitely. Nothing is deleted for 12 months, and we email before anything is.

Do you charge per seat?

No. The price is per workspace, so inviting a reviewer, a contractor or a stakeholder costs nothing extra.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes, and it follows your operating system by default. This page is in it right now if your system is — including the pricing cards, where the recommended plan is raised rather than recoloured so the emphasis survives the theme change.

How accessible is it, really?

Every colour pairing is contrast-audited in four themes as a build gate, keyboard behaviour is tested against a real browser, and the build fails rather than shipping a regression. The accessibility page lists what is verified and what is not.
