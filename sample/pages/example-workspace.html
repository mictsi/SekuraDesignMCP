Team guide

This is our shared starting point for clear decisions, useful documentation and a workspace that everyone can navigate.

Make work easy to find

Give each project one home. Keep its purpose, owner and next decision together. Link to the source instead of keeping competing copies.

  • Use a short title that describes the work.
  • Keep the latest decision near the top.
  • Write down the context someone joining tomorrow will need.
